# Workflow Questionnaire — Tobacco Commerce Business

> Goal: Understand your daily operations in enough detail to design an AI assistant that handles order intake and inventory tracking. Be as specific and honest as possible — the messier the reality, the more useful the answer.

---

## Section 1: Your Business at a Glance

1. How many employees do you have (including yourself)?
2. What are their roles? (e.g., packaging, shipping, sales, admin, accounting)
3. Roughly how many active customers do you have? (a range is fine)
4. What's the split between wholesale clients and retail shop clients? (e.g., 70/30, 50/50)
5. How many different products (SKUs) do you carry approximately?
6. Do you also sell online (website, marketplace) or only through direct orders?

---

## Section 2: How Orders Come In

7. What channels do customers use to place orders? Rank them from most to least frequent:
   - WhatsApp
   - Phone calls
   - Email
   - In person
   - Fax
   - Web portal / website
   - Other (specify)

8. Roughly how many orders do you receive per day? (average day vs. peak day)

9. For WhatsApp orders specifically:
   - Can you screenshot or describe 3-4 typical messages you receive? (hide customer names if needed). I'm looking for how structured they are:
     - Do customers send product names/codes and quantities?
     - Do they send photos of handwritten lists?
     - Do they send voice notes?
     - Do they say things like "il solito" (the usual) or "come l'ultima volta"?
   - Do customers negotiate prices via WhatsApp? How often?
   - Do you respond with availability/confirmation on WhatsApp, or just take the order?

10. For phone call orders:
    - Do you write down the order during the call? Where? (paper, phone notes, directly into Excel?)
    - How long does a typical order call take?
    - Do customers negotiate on the phone?

11. For email orders:
    - Are they more structured than WhatsApp? (e.g., do some clients send a proper order list?)
    - Do any clients send PDF or Excel order files?

12. Do repeat customers tend to order the same things? What percentage would you say are "routine reorders" vs. genuinely new/different orders?

13. How do you currently confirm an order to the customer? (WhatsApp reply, email, phone call, nothing — you just ship it?)

## Section 3: Pricing and Negotiations

14. Do you have a standard price list, or does pricing vary by customer?
15. If pricing varies:
    - How many price tiers or customer categories do you have?
    - Where is the pricing information stored? (in your head, in Excel, printed lists?)
    - What triggers a different price? (volume, relationship, customer type, negotiation?)
16. How often do customers try to negotiate a discount? (every order, sometimes, rarely?)
17. When they negotiate, who decides — only you, or can employees also give discounts?
18. How often do your purchase prices from suppliers change? How do you update your own prices when they do?

## Section 4: Inventory and Stock

19. Describe your Excel inventory system:
    - What columns do you track? (product name, code, quantity, purchase price, sale price, location, expiry date?)
    - How many rows / products are in it?
    - How often do you update it? (after every order? end of day? when you remember?)
    - Do you have one file or multiple? (e.g., separate sheets for different product categories?)

20. How often is the Excel out of sync with reality? (e.g., you sell something but forget to update)
21. How do you know when to reorder from your suppliers?
    - Do you have minimum stock levels set up, or do you reorder when you notice something is low?
    - Have you ever run out of a product and lost a sale because of it?
    - Have you ever over-ordered and been stuck with excess stock?
22. Do any of your products have expiry dates you need to track?
23. Where is your inventory physically? One warehouse, multiple locations, or mixed with a showroom?

## Section 10: Follow-Up Questions (from transcript review)

### Accounting System

    - How much does Iconta cost (monthly/annual)?

52. How many direct-import competitors are there in your market? How much cheaper are they typically?

## How to Answer

- Don't overthink it. First-draft, honest answers are more useful than polished ones.
- If the answer is "I don't know" or "it depends," say that — and explain what it depends on.
- Real examples beat generalizations. Instead of "customers send orders by WhatsApp," describe or screenshot an actual message.
- If something feels embarrassing (e.g., "my Excel is a disaster"), that's exactly what I need to know.
